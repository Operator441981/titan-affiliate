Privacy Policy

TITAN Affiliate. Last updated: September 19, 2026

Who we are

TITAN Affiliate is an independent developer project operated by Justin Swan in Louisiana, USA. It is not a company with employees or customers.

What the application does

The application connects to Pinterest's API using the developer's own Pinterest account. It reads Pinterest's publicly available trend data (trending keywords) to help choose topics for Pins. Later versions may publish Pins to that same account.

Data we access

Only the trend data and account permissions granted by the account owner through Pinterest's OAuth authorization. The application does not access, collect, or store personal information about other Pinterest users, and it does not build profiles of individuals.

Data we store

OAuth access and refresh tokens and trend keyword results are stored locally on the developer's own computer. Tokens are kept in a local configuration file that is not published or shared.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share data with third parties. Pins created by the application may contain affiliate links, and visitors who follow them are subject to the privacy policies of Pinterest and the linked retailer.

Data retention and deletion

Trend results are kept only as long as needed to choose topics. The account owner can revoke the application's access at any time in Pinterest account settings, and tokens can be deleted from the local configuration file.

Children

The application is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest revision.
